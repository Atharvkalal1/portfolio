Gradient descent in 3D

Four optimizers start from the same point on the same loss surface and race to the lowest point. Drag to rotate, click the surface to move the start, then compare their paths, the floor shadows and the loss chart.

How to tell them apart

Deep learning

A convolution slides a small kernel over an image and measures how well each patch matches it. Paint on the input, pick or edit a kernel (click its cells), then watch the feature map fill in. ReLU keeps positive matches and max pooling shrinks the map.

Transformer attention

In self-attention every word asks “who should I listen to?” and softmax turns the answers into weights. Hover a word on the top row. Switch heads to see different roles, and lower the temperature to sharpen the focus.

Embeddings

Words become points in a vector space, and similar meanings land close together. Drag to rotate, click a point or type a word, then watch a nearest-neighbour search light up its closest matches.

RAG pipeline

Retrieval-augmented generation looks things up before it answers. Ask about my work and watch the question get embedded, matched against a small knowledge base, packed into a prompt and answered. Switch retrieval off to see why grounding matters.
